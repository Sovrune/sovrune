# Launch kit

## Positioning

**Headline:** The open-source operating system for AI-run companies.

**One line:** Sovrune connects company evidence to strategy, coordinated AI work, human-approved delivery, and measured outcomes.

## Founding design partner

- $2,500 onboarding
- $500/month managed operation
- Customer supplies model-provider usage
- One managed deployment, one company adapter, two integrations, model configuration, weekly founder support

## Short-form video briefs

### 1. Busy is not better

**Hook:** “Your AI agents shipped twelve tasks. Did the company actually improve?”

Show disconnected task cards, then the Sovrune circuit: Signal → Strategy → Work → Approval → Outcome. End card: “Sovrune is open source.”

### 2. Bring any model

**Hook:** “Your company operating system should not belong to one AI provider.”

Show Strategy/Anthropic, Research/Gemini, Engineering/OpenAI-compatible, Sensitive analysis/Ollama. End card: “Bring your models. Keep your data.”

### 3. The complete loop

**Hook:** “Most AI tools stop when the answer appears.”

Animate one business signal through a decision, reviewable work, human approval, and a measured verdict. End card: “Run Sovrune locally.”

## Launch posts

### GitHub / LinkedIn

Today we are releasing Sovrune, an open-source operating system for AI-run companies. Agent harnesses help agents work; Sovrune helps a company decide what matters and measure whether the work succeeded. It is self-hosted, model-neutral, and intentionally stops at the human approval boundary. The first alpha is live with a fictional company adapter and support for hosted or local models.

### Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: Sovrune – an open-source operating system for AI-run companies

We built Sovrune to connect sourced business state to strategy, specialist AI offices, human-approved delivery, and outcome measurement. The alpha is intentionally small, self-hosted, and provider-neutral. I would value feedback on the Company Adapter contract and accountability loop.
